Explore the methodology → See the key concepts

The central idea

An education that follows the child

More than a teaching method, Montessori is a way of understanding human development — grounded in scientific observation, in respect for each child's pace, and in the belief in their natural capacity to learn.

The child at the center

The environment and the adult adapt to the child — not the other way around. The child is the active protagonist of their own formation.

Scientific observation

A physician and scientist, Maria Montessori built her method by observing how children actually learn.

Autonomy and freedom

With freedom within clear limits, the child develops independence, concentration and self-discipline.

“Help me to do it myself.” — The essential principle of Montessori education
