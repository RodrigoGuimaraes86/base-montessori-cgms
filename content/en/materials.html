The concrete before the abstract

The Montessori Materials

Carefully designed by Maria Montessori, the materials are concrete, beautiful and self-correcting objects. Each one isolates a single quality to be learned and allows the child to discover on their own — with their hands.

What makes a material "Montessori"

  • Isolation of difficulty — each material works on a single concept at a time.
  • Control of error — the child themselves perceives and corrects their mistakes.
  • From concrete to abstract — the abstract idea is first experienced through the hands.
  • Beauty and simplicity — attractive, well-crafted materials that invite use.
  • Purpose — each object has a clear developmental aim.

By area

Practical life

Dressing frames (buttons, bows, zippers), pouring of liquids and grains, cleaning and caring for the environment. They develop motor coordination, concentration, order and independence.

Sensorial

The Pink Tower, Brown Stair, knobbed cylinders, red rods, color boxes, tactile tablets and sound boxes. They refine the senses and help the child order the world.

Language

Sandpaper letters, the movable alphabet, objects and images to expand vocabulary. The child feels the shape of the letters, composes words and moves naturally toward reading and writing.

Mathematics

Number rods, spindle boxes, golden beads and the decimal system. Quantity and operations become tangible before they turn into symbols on paper.

Culture and sciences

Puzzle globes and maps, continent puzzles, botany and zoology materials, timelines. They broaden the child's view of the world and awaken interest in the universe.
