References

The content of this portal is written in our own words and grounded in the sources below — the writings of Maria Montessori (primary sources) and the studies and commentators that interpret her work (secondary sources).

  2. Montessori, Maria. Citizen of the World: Key Montessori Readings — includes "The Forgotten Citizen" (UNESCO, 1951).
  3. Montessori, Maria. The Discovery of the Child.
  4. Montessori, Maria. The Secret of Childhood.
  5. Montessori, Maria. The Absorbent Mind.
  6. Montessori, Maria. From Childhood to Adolescence.
  7. Montessori, Maria. To Educate the Human Potential.
  8. Montessori, Maria. Education for a New World.
  9. Montessori, Maria. The Advanced Montessori Method, Volume I (Spontaneous Activity in Education) — montessori150.org.
  10. Montessori, Maria. Education and Peace.
  11. Montessori, Maria. The Child in the Family.
  12. Montessori, Maria; Yesson, Heather (ed.). The Child, Society and the World: A Selection of Speeches and Writings.
  13. Montessori, Maria. Maria Montessori Speaks to Parents.
  14. Montessori, Maria. “Some Suggestions and Remarks Upon Observing Children” (London Course, 1921, Lecture 3).

Many of Maria Montessori's works are in the public domain and can be read online (for example, at Project Gutenberg and the Internet Archive), or found in the AMI book catalogue at montessori150.org.
